Brake Repair Services in Lakewood, CO

Your brakes are your vehicle’s most critical safety system. When they start to wear down, stopping distances increase and your peace of mind decreases. At Four Seasons Automotive, our expert technicians provide thorough brake inspections and precision repairs using high-quality parts to keep your brake system responsive.

Your vehicle’s brakes operate through a complex system to slow down or stop your car safely. Most passenger cars and light trucks use a hydraulic brake system. When you press the brake pedal, it activates a master cylinder that sends brake fluid through lines to the calipers, which clamp the brake pads against the rotors (or brake shoes against the drums if you have drum brakes), generating the necessary friction to slow the wheels.

Signs My Vehicle Needs Brake Repairs

  • Unusual Noises: Squealing or grinding sounds often suggest worn brake pads. Worn pads can cause further damage if left unaddressed.
  • Poor Responsiveness: If your brakes feel “spongy” or require more effort to engage, this may indicate air in the brake lines or a problem with the master cylinder.
  • Vibrating Brake Pedal: A vibrating pedal can be a sign of warped rotors causing uneven friction during braking. It often needs rotor resurfacing or replacement.
  • Pulling to One Side: If the vehicle veers left or right upon braking, this could mean a caliper is stuck or there is uneven wear on the brake pads.

Should you need repair services, Four Seasons Automotive employs experienced technicians to handle everything from brake pad and rotor replacements to caliper and master cylinder repairs, keeping safety at the forefront.
