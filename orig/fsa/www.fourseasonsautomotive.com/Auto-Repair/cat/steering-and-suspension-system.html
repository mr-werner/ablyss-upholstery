The Importance of Your Vehicle’s Steering and Suspension Systems

The steering system is responsible for directing your vehicle's wheels, allowing you to navigate roads with precision. Meanwhile, the suspension system supports the vehicle's weight, absorbs shocks from the road, and maintains tire contact for optimal traction. Together, these systems ensure stability and handling performance.

What Steering and Suspension System Services Entail

Regular service of the steering and suspension system involves inspecting components such as shock absorbers, struts, ball joints, and tie rods. Our expert technicians at Four Seasons Automotive will perform thorough checks and necessary adjustments or replacements to keep your vehicle in top condition.

Signs My Vehicle Needs Steering and Suspension System Service

  • Unusual noises when driving over bumps
  • Vehicle pulling to one side
  • Uneven tire wear
  • Excessive bouncing after hitting a bump
  • Difficulty steering or a loose steering wheel
