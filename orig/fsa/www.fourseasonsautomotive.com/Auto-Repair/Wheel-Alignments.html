Wheel Alignments in Lakewood, CO

Four Seasons Automotive is your local expert for precision wheel alignments — keeping your vehicle driving straight, your tires lasting longer, and your wallet from taking an unnecessary hit at the pump.

Why Wheel Alignment Matters

Every pothole, curb tap, and hard stop nudges your wheels slightly out of position. Over time, even minor misalignment puts uneven stress on your tires, forces your engine to work harder, and can quietly compromise your steering and suspension. The result? Faster tire wear, worse fuel economy, and a vehicle that pulls when you just want it to go straight.

At Four Seasons Automotive, we use advanced alignment equipment to measure and correct camber, caster, and toe angles to your vehicle manufacturer's exact specifications — so you drive away with confidence every time.

Signs Your Vehicle Needs an Alignment

  • Your car pulls to the left or right even when you're holding the wheel steady
  • Uneven or rapid tire wear on one side or across the tread
  • A crooked steering wheel while driving on a straight road
  • Vibration or drifting at highway speeds

Fast, Accurate Alignments from a Team You Can Trust

Don't let a small alignment issue snowball into a costly tire replacement or suspension repair. Four Seasons Automotive gets it right the first time — with honest service, transparent pricing, and technicians who genuinely care about how your vehicle performs.
