Volkswagen Brake Repair and Service in Denver, CO

Owning a Volkswagen can be a very enjoyable experience for you, especially if you are owning a car for the first time. At Paddock Imports, you can get all of your brake and repair services at our location in Denver, whether you need to replace your brake fluid, rotors, brake pads, and more. At Paddock Imports, our goal is to get you back on the road safely. Worn or damaged brakes can put you, your passengers, and other people in dangerous situations, so it’s important to keep your entire braking system in great condition. This is especially true here in Denver, where snowy conditions can compromise traction.

When Do I Need Brake Service?

When you know the signs of brake problems, you can do what it takes to address any issues quickly. Here are some of the most important things to look for as you ensure that you’re keeping your vehicle safe no matter what kind of weather the Rocky Mountains throw our way:

  • If your brake pedal starts to feel soft, it could be a sign of pad wear, the presence of air, or moisture in the brake hydraulic system.
  • If you are experiencing a shimmy or vibration while applying the brakes that could be an indication that the brake rotors are warped and require service.
  • Your car doesn’t stop as sharply as it used to. This is a clear sign that it’s time for brake service. The ability to stop on a dime is an important part of the driving experience, and it’s also important for your safety.
  • If your brakes squeal, screech, or grind allow us to provide a free inspection
