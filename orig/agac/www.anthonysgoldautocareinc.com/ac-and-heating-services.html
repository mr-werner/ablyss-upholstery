Air Conditioning Service

An auto air conditioner loses around five percent of its efficiency per year without routine maintenance. Without regular maintenance, your air conditioning system could be running poorly. Our A/C technicians can help regain the lost efficiency. We are trained and skilled in all aspects of AC repair, from advanced computerized parts to environmental disposal applications. Today's AC systems are complex, and new improvements are always being originated. Choose our seasoned team for exceptional air conditioning services

Heating Service

Keep your automobile warm during the cold Colorado winter with regular service and professional repairs from our highly skilled team at Anthony's Gold Auto Care Inc. There are many signs that the heater in your vehicle needs repairs, and those include:

  • Terrible Smell Coming Out Of Heater Vents
  • Windshield & Window Fog Up 
  • Leaking Antifreeze
  • Excessive Amount Of Coolant
